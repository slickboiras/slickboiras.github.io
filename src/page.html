About

01Who I am,
how I work

Half designer, half engineer. I make posters, key visuals and event photos, and I build circuits, firmware and software. I'd rather do both than pick one.

Background

I've spent three years on publication and documentation (PDD) committees, making posters and key visuals and shooting events on a Sony a6000. At BINUS University I study Computer Engineering, from analog circuits to microcontrollers.

How I approach technology

First principles come first: the signal path, the timing, the power budget. Code comes after I understand the hardware. Then I design the part people touch, because a device that works but confuses people still needs work.
